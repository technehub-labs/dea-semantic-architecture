# Tenets — Enterprise Semantic Architecture

> **Status:** placeholder. Full prose lands in Phase 1 of [CR-ESA-01](./change-requests/CR-ESA-01.md).

ESA tenets are **axiom-derived**, not invented. Every tenet traces to one or more cells of the ECF 7×7 axiom grid (`technehub-labs/dea-metaframework`), or is explicitly flagged as a sector-specific extension (deferred to CR-ESA-02 onward).

## Phase 1 deliverable targets

- Three tenets, each with:
  - **Statement** — the tenet itself.
  - **Axiom source** — ECF cell(s) it derives from.
  - **Operational rule** — how the tenet becomes enforceable in day-to-day ESA practice.
  - **Anti-pattern** — what violates the tenet.
- Cross-references to CR-ESA-01, OpenDEAM, ECF, Concepts Model.

## Three placeholder tenets (final wording in Phase 1)

1. **Semantic layers are architectural, not accidental.** The organisation of meaning into layers (terms → vocabulary → taxonomy → thesaurus → ontology → knowledge graph) is an explicit design choice, not an emergent property.
2. **Vocabulary governance is a first-class discipline.** Vocabularies are versioned, deprecated, mapped, and retired; they are never silently evolved.
3. **Concept graphs are graphs, not hierarchies.** A concept graph admits broader/narrower, related, equivalent, and mapped-from edges; collapsing it to a tree loses semantic fidelity.
